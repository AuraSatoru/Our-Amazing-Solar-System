Planets

The Sun

The Sun is the star at the center of our solar system. It is a nearly perfect sphere of hot plasma, heated to incandescence by nuclear fusion reactions in its core. The Sun provides the energy necessary for life on Earth and drives our climate and weather.

Mercury

Mercury is the smallest planet in our solar system and the closest planet to the Sun. It has a rocky surface covered with craters and experiences extreme temperature changes between day and night.

Venus

Venus is the second planet from the Sun and the hottest planet in our solar system. Its thick atmosphere is mostly carbon dioxide, creating a powerful greenhouse effect and yellowish clouds of sulfuric acid.

Earth

Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the only astronomical object known to harbor life. It has a diverse climate and geography, with oceans, mountains, and forests. Earth’s atmosphere protects life from harmful solar radiation and helps regulate temperature.

Mars

Mars is the fourth planet from the Sun and is often called the Red Planet because of the iron minerals in its soil. It has a thin atmosphere, dusty plains, deep valleys, and the largest volcano in the solar system.

Jupiter

Jupiter is the largest planet in our solar system and the fifth planet from the Sun. It is a gas giant with colorful cloud bands, a powerful magnetic field, and a Great Red Spot that is a long-lasting storm.

Saturn

Saturn is the sixth planet from the Sun and the second-largest planet in our solar system. It is a gas giant best known for its bright, extensive system of icy rings.

Uranus

Uranus is the seventh planet from the Sun and an ice giant. It rotates on its side and has a blue-green atmosphere, faint rings, and many moons.

Neptune

Neptune is the eighth and most distant planet from the Sun. It is an ice giant with a deep blue atmosphere, powerful winds, and faint rings.

Moons

Moon

The Moon is Earth's only natural satellite. Its gravity helps create ocean tides, and its surface is covered with craters, mountains, and dusty plains.

Phobos

Phobos is the larger and closer of Mars's two moons. It is an irregular, cratered moon that orbits Mars very quickly and is slowly moving closer to the planet.

Titan

Titan is Saturn's largest moon and the second-largest moon in the solar system. It has a thick atmosphere and lakes and seas of liquid methane and ethane on its surface.

Titania

Titania is the largest moon of Uranus. It is an icy, rocky world with a dark surface, large valleys, and many impact craters.

Missions

Apollo 1

Apollo 1 was the first crewed Apollo mission. A cabin fire during a ground test in 1967 took the lives of astronauts Gus Grissom, Ed White, and Roger Chaffee and led to major safety improvements.

Apollo 11

Apollo 11 was the first crewed mission to land on the Moon. In 1969, Neil Armstrong and Buzz Aldrin walked on the lunar surface while Michael Collins orbited above.

Apollo 13

Apollo 13 was planned as a Moon-landing mission, but an oxygen-tank explosion forced the crew to abort the landing. The astronauts returned safely to Earth after a remarkable rescue effort.

Chandrayaan-3

Chandrayaan-3 was an Indian Space Research Organisation mission that successfully landed near the Moon's south polar region in 2023 and deployed the Pragyan rover.

Gaganyaan

Gaganyaan is India's human spaceflight programme. Its goal is to send Indian astronauts into low Earth orbit and return them safely to Earth using an Indian launch vehicle and crew module.

Aditya-L1

Aditya-L1 is India's first space-based solar observatory. It studies the Sun from a special point near the Earth-Sun system to observe solar activity and space weather.

Luna 25

Luna 25 was a Roscosmos lunar mission launched in 2023 to study the Moon's south polar region and test landing and sampling technologies.
